Bulk & repeat orders

Contractors and developers get bulk discounts on repeat orders. Share your bill of quantities on WhatsApp and we will quote it line by line.

Cut-to-size service

Installation available

Delivery & coverage

We deliver to your site, not to a stage

Small loads go out on our own pick-up and boda network; larger project orders are consolidated and delivered directly to site with a delivery note you can check against your list.

  • Nairobi & environs: same-day or next-morning delivery on stocked lines.
  • Nakuru, Naivasha, Thika, Juja, Ruiru, Ngong, Kajiado: 24 – 48 hours.
  • Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nyeri: 2 – 4 days by courier or bus parcel.
  • Offloading help arranged for heavy board and tile orders on request.
