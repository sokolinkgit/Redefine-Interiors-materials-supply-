Our process

How every project runs

01

Enquiry on WhatsApp or phone

Send photos, drawings or dimensions. We reply with options, a price guide and the earliest start date.

02

Free site visit & measurement

We measure precisely, check services (water, power, drainage), note access and take photos for the workshop.

03

Itemised quotation & materials approval

Written breakdown plus physical samples. You approve materials and the start date before any deposit is paid.

04

Workshop fabrication

Boards cut, edge-banded and assembled in our workshop, aluminium welded and gypsum pre-assembled where possible — keeping site time short.

05

Installation & daily clean-up

Our installers work to the agreed schedule, protect your floors, and clear debris at the end of every day.

06

Handover, snag list & guarantee

We walk through the work with you, fix anything on your snag list, then hand over your 12-month workmanship guarantee and care notes.

Our promise

What you can hold us to

12-month guarantee

Any failure in our workmanship — a sagging shelf, a sticking door, a leaking joint — is fixed free within twelve months.

Fixed quotation

The figure we agree is the figure you pay. Variations only happen if you change the scope, and always in writing first.

Dates we keep

We book realistic dates and tell you immediately if anything threatens them. Late surprise handovers are not our style.

No material switching

The laminate, quartz, profile and hardware you approve is what gets installed. Samples stay with you for reference.

Countrywide service

Nairobi, Nakuru, Naivasha, Thika, Juja, Ruiru, Ngong, Machakos, Kajiado, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret — and everywhere between.

One point of contact

The person who quoted your job is the person who answers your WhatsApp — from measurement to handover and after.
