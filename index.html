Why Redefine Interiors & Materials Supply

The contractor who actually shows up

Bad interiors work in Kenya is rarely about talent — it is about materials that were swapped, measurements rushed and timelines that move. We built Redefine Interiors to fix exactly that.

  • Site visit and measurement — you get a written, itemised quotation, not a rough guess.
  • We bring samples of the boards, laminates, quartz and profiles so you approve before fabrication starts.
  • Our own carpenters, aluminium welders, gypsum fixers and tilers — no mystery subcontractors.
  • Milestone payments with receipts, and a workmanship guarantee on completion.

Every kitchen starts with a site measurement. You then receive an itemised written quotation covering carcasses, doors, worktops and fittings — the figure depends on the length of your kitchen run, the worktop you choose and the internals. No commitment until you approve it.
