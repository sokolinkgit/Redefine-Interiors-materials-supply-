Why Redefine

The contractor who actually shows up

Bad interiors work in Kenya is rarely about talent — it is about materials that were swapped, measurements rushed and timelines that move. We built Redefine Interiors to fix exactly that.

  • Free site visit and measurement — you get a written, itemised quotation, not a rough guess.
  • We bring samples of the boards, laminates, quartz and profiles so you approve before fabrication starts.
  • Our own carpenters, aluminium welders, gypsum fixers and tilers — no mystery subcontractors.
  • Milestone payments with receipts, and a 12-month workmanship guarantee on completion.

How it works

From WhatsApp message to handover

01

Send us your idea on WhatsApp

Share photos, a Pinterest link, or just your room dimensions. Tell us your location and budget range and we reply with options and a price guide the same working day.

02

Free site visit & measurement

Our technician visits, measures precisely (walls are never square), checks plumbing and power points, and helps you choose materials from physical samples.

03

Itemised quotation & 3D layout

You receive a written breakdown — carcasses, doors, worktops, fittings, labour and transport — plus a layout drawing so you know exactly what you are paying for.

04

Fabrication, installation & handover

Work happens at our workshop, then installation happens on site on the agreed dates. We clean up, walk you through everything, and back it with a 12-month guarantee.

Answers before you ask

Still unsure about something? Send us a WhatsApp message — we answer pricing questions with real numbers, not guesswork.

Ask a question

Our kitchen cabinet packages start from about KES 185,000 including carcasses, doors, worktops and fittings. The final figure depends on the length of your kitchen run, the worktop you choose and the internals — we give an itemised quotation after a free site measurement.

Yes. We supply MDF, laminate, gypsum, aluminium, tiles, hardware and fittings at affordable wholesale-friendly prices, with delivery to your site anywhere in Kenya. You can also hire our installation team if you prefer.

Site visits and measurements are free within Nairobi and the surrounding areas such as Ruiru, Juja, Ngong and Kiambu. For Nakuru, Naivasha, Thika and other counties we charge a small transport fee that is deducted from your project once you award us the work.

Wardrobes normally take 7–14 days, kitchens 2–4 weeks, gypsum ceilings 3–9 days and full shop renovations 3–6 weeks from the day materials are approved. We agree the timeline in writing before starting.

We work on a clear milestone basis: a deposit to buy materials and start fabrication, then progressive payments as agreed in your contract. Every payment is receipted and itemised.

Yes — we serve all parts of Kenya. We regularly complete projects in Nakuru, Naivasha, Ngong, Juja, Ruiru, Thika, Machakos, Kajiado, Eldoret and Mombasa. Distance affects transport cost only, never the quality.
