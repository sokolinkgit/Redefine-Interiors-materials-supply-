Transparent pricing

What changes the final figure

We would rather explain pricing than surprise you later. These are the five things that move a quotation up or down.

Size & layout

A 2.4m straight kitchen run costs far less than a 4.8m U-shape with an island. Everything is priced per linear metre or per square metre.

Finish you choose

Standard melamine laminate vs high-gloss acrylic, matt or textured doors, veneer, quartz vs granite vs solid wood worktops.

Hardware quality

Soft-close hinges and heavy-duty runners cost more than basic fittings — but they are the difference between five years and fifteen.

Location & access

Transport beyond Nairobi and stairs or tight access affect labour. We tell you the transport figure upfront, in writing.

Scope of work

Cabinet only, or cabinet plus plumbing, electrical points, tiling, ceiling and painting? Bundling always reduces the total.

Timeline

Rush jobs with weekend and night work attract a premium. Normal scheduling keeps your cost at our standard rate.
