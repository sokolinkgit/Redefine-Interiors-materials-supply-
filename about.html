Our story

It started with one kitchen in Kahawa

In 2014 we rebuilt a family kitchen in Kahawa West that two other contractors had abandoned mid-job. The client referred us to her sister in Githurai; her brother needed wardrobes in Kasarani; a friend wanted a shop ceiling in Thika. Twelve years later that referral chain has become 480+ completed projects across Kenya.

We grew slowly on purpose. Instead of becoming brokers who subcontract everything, we trained our own carpenters, aluminium welders, gypsum fixers and tilers — and we added a materials supply arm so clients could buy genuine boards, profiles and hardware at fair prices, whether they hired us to install or not.

Today we work from Nairobi and deliver anywhere in the country. What has not changed is the standard: measured properly, built honestly, handed over on time.

What guides us

Five rules we do not break

Measure twice, cut once

Every project starts with a physical site measurement — never a phone estimate. Walls in Kenyan homes are rarely square, and guessing costs more than checking.

Quote honestly

We would rather lose a job than win it with an unrealistic price and recover the difference through “extras”. Our quotations are itemised and our transport is stated upfront.

Materials you approve

You see and touch the board, laminate, quartz and profiles before we build. What you approve is what we install — no substitutions, ever.

Our people, paid properly

Skilled artisans paid fairly do careful work. We use our own crew, we train apprentices, and we do not disappear when a job is finished.

We come back

A 12-month workmanship guarantee is not marketing — it is a promise that we will return and fix any fault in our work, free, no arguments.

Distance is not an excuse

Naivasha, Nakuru, Thika, Juja, Ruiru, Ngong, Machakos, Kajiado — we travel, we deliver and we finish. Kenya is our workshop floor.

Quality standards

What “done properly” actually means here

  • 18mm boards for carcasses and doors — never compressed 15mm sold as 18mm.
  • Moisture-resistant board in kitchens, bathrooms and any unit near a sink.
  • Edge banding on every visible cut edge, including the shelves you rarely see.
  • Soft-close hinges and rated runners — no basic fittings hidden behind a nice door.
  • Gypsum fixed to a properly levelled metal grid, taped, filled and sanded flat.
  • Aluminium fabricated square, sealed against rain, with locking gear that works.
  • Floors and doorways protected during installation; site cleaned before handover.
  • A written snag list resolved before we ask for the final payment.
